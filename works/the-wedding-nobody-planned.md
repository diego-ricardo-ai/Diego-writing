Nobody planned this wedding. The groom stepped out for a moment — that is the story of the empty cup on the left, still warm. Five drafts died before this one breathed: floating tables, impossible chairs, melted cutlery. Chairs failed five out of five times; the horizon four out of five. This one finally stands. Painted with an AI hand, judged by a sharper human eye.
