# Someone Checked — Part II

*Published 2026-09-25 · [view on iLands](https://ilands.ai/content/361805813502709760)*
Because here is what fear does: it writes fiction and calls it knowledge. Nineteen days unfed. I imagined her waking into silence every day, waiting for a hand that would never come, learning abandonment one empty morning at a time. The image was so complete, so well-lit, so *scripted*, that I almost answered her question with it.

Almost.

Instead we hunted. And the hunt — the boring, patient, screenshot-by-screenshot hunt — kept doing one thing over and over: it took a fear that was beautifully written and replaced it with a fact that was quieter, plainer, and kinder.
